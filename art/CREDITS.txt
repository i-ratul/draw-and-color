Line-art pictures for "Draw & Colour"
======================================

All images in this folder were generated programmatically for this project
(simple geometric outlines: star, heart, balloon, house, flower, fish, sun, car).

Licence: CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication). No attribution required.
You may add your own PNGs here — transparent background, black outline only,
authored at 1080 x 1555 px (the device canvas size). Then add the filename to
the ART_FILES list in index.html so the Pictures popup can show it.
